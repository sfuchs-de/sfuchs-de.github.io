02 · The series

Built around connected questions

The sessions bring together junior scholars and new papers that treat geography as central to economic adjustment. The program began with two spatial-economics sessions in 2021 and has grown into a wider conversation spanning cities, mobility, infrastructure, trade, public economics, and networks.

Convened by Simon Fuchs and Chris Severen, with collaborators across editions.
